Frequently Asked Questions

Sign-in is failing. What should I do?

Check your connection first, then try another sign-in method (Apple, Google, or email code). If only one method fails, tell us the time it happened and we will check server logs. If you see “account has been merged”, that identity is already bound to another account — sign in with the original account instead.

My subscription is not active, or I don't see it on a new device

Tap “Restore Purchases” on the subscription page and confirm you are using the same Apple ID. If it still fails, send us a screenshot of your purchase history (App Store → Account → Purchase History).

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iOS Settings → tap your Apple ID at the top → Subscriptions → arXivChat → Cancel Subscription. Your benefits remain until the end of the current period and will not renew. Deleting the App does not cancel the subscription.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to submit a request. We cannot process refunds directly.

I've hit the AI quota limit

Summaries, AI guides, translation, daily briefs, and PDF processing have a daily quota for non-members, which resets each day. A subscription removes the limit.

AI answers or translations look wrong

AI output can contain errors — always defer to the original paper. Send us the paper ID and the issue to help us improve.

A PDF won't open or fails to download

Check your connection and available storage. If the paper is not accessible on arXiv, we cannot retrieve it either. Try “Open original in browser” on the paper detail page.

My favorites or reading history disappeared

Make sure you are signed in to the same account and that iCloud sync is enabled in Settings. Signing out clears the local cache; signing back in re-syncs from the server.

How do I delete my account and all data?

Open Settings → Account Management → Delete Account. Deletion is permanent and server-side data is removed within a reasonable period. Note: deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription — cancel it separately in iOS subscription settings.

I'm not receiving push notifications

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → arXivChat, and make sure the relevant categories (daily updates, translation finished, trending papers) are enabled in the App's notification settings.

Which languages and devices are supported?

Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, and Indonesian. Works on iPhone and iPad, iOS 14 or later.
