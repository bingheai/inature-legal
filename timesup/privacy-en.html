TimesUp Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-06
Operator: Nanjing Zhuoben Technology Co., Ltd
Contact: arxivchat@gmail.com

Scope

TimesUp lets a parent set Screen Time rules on the iPhone or iPad being managed. Devices are authorized and managed separately. There is no TimesUp login, remote pairing, location tracking or aggregation of usage totals across devices.

Information kept on the device

TimesUp stores system-provided tokens for selected apps, categories or websites; pause state; daily allowances and reset time; a device display name; language and appearance preferences; and local enforcement state. The app and its extensions share relevant settings locally through their App Group. These Screen Time rules and selection tokens are not uploaded to our services or to RevenueCat.

Parent PIN and recovery-code verifiers are salted hashes stored in the device Keychain, not plaintext preferences. We cannot read your original PIN or recovery code or retrieve them remotely from a server.

Apple and RevenueCat purchase processing

The free trial and lifetime unlock use Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple handles purchase confirmation and payment information. TimesUp uses StoreKit and RevenueCat to verify purchases, restore access, prevent purchase abuse and analyze purchases.

RevenueCat uses an anonymous App User ID and purchase-related information such as transactions, products, entitlements, storefront and currency. TimesUp currently does not set a name, email address, identifiable custom user ID or advertising identifier. Screen Time rules, parent PINs, recovery codes and voice profiles are not sent as purchase attributes.

Apple and RevenueCat process purchase information under their services, privacy policies and applicable retention requirements. We do not receive your payment card number or Apple Account password. Purchase data may be processed outside your country or region, subject to the providers' published policies and actual service configuration.

Apple privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

Optional microphone, voice and biometrics

Microphone access is requested only when you choose voice enrollment or verification. Voice comparison takes place on the device; temporary recordings are cleaned up after processing. Voice features are kept in the device Keychain and can be deleted in Parent security settings. They are not uploaded to Apple or RevenueCat for this feature.

Voice match is optional and experimental. It does not verify spoken words or resist recording replay and may accept another voice. It is not a strong identity guarantee. The parent PIN remains available.

Optional Face ID or Touch ID uses the system authentication interface. TimesUp does not receive Apple's face or fingerprint templates. Enable this only if the device's enrolled biometrics belong to a parent. It is off by default.

Information and advertising not used

TimesUp does not request access to location, contacts or the photo library, or read conversations through this app. The current version has no third-party advertising or cross-company advertising tracking. We will update disclosures and obtain permission where needed if these practices change.

Parents and children

The app is set up and managed by a parent. Child or teen Screen Time authorization follows Apple's system process and applicable guardian-approval requirements. TimesUp does not ask children to submit a name, email address or phone number, and does not provide social messaging or public user-generated content.

Retention, deletion and your choices

A parent can clear all rules to stop monitoring and remove restrictions, delete a voice profile, disable quick unlock and manage permissions. Clearing rules preserves the parent PIN and purchase history.

Deleting the app does not necessarily remove Keychain records or Apple's and RevenueCat's transaction history. Purchase records and trial eligibility may still be used to restore original access. Contact us about purchase-data or support-data requests. We may need reasonable purchase-ownership information, but do not send a parent PIN, recovery code, full payment-card details or Apple Account password.

If you contact support, we process the email address, issue description and necessary order information you provide to answer your request, resolve issues and meet applicable retention requirements.

Website hosting

This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard request information such as IP addresses under its privacy statement. We have not added advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies to this website.

GitHub privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Changes and contact

We will update this policy and its effective date when features or data practices change. Contact arxivchat@gmail.com with questions about this policy or your data.
