Getting started

Install TimesUp on the iPhone or iPad you want to manage. A parent creates a separate 6-digit PIN, saves a recovery code and completes Apple's Screen Time authorization. Child or teen authorization requires an eligible Apple Account, Family Sharing and guardian approval.

Enter Parent controls, claim the free trial or restore existing access, choose managed apps, set a daily allowance and save your changes.

Will the trial charge me automatically?

No. The trial claimed through Apple lasts 30 days. There is no subscription or automatic renewal. A parent may separately choose a lifetime purchase at the price shown by the App Store.

Why is an app still restricted after I end a manual pause?

Manual pause and the daily allowance are separate limits. Ending a manual pause does not clear an exhausted daily allowance. To remove all restrictions, authenticate as a parent, choose “Resume or clear limits” on the paywall and confirm “Clear all rules” in restricted management. This does not require a purchase. It stops monitoring and removes rules while preserving the parent PIN and purchase history.

Restoring purchases

Use the Apple Account that originally claimed or purchased access. Choose Restore purchases on the paywall or in Settings > About TimesUp. Restoring an expired trial does not start a new one. IAP Family Sharing is currently disabled; Family Sharing authorization does not automatically share the lifetime purchase between different Apple Accounts.

Authorization problems

Check that you are running iOS/iPadOS 17.4 or later and that the Apple Account, Family Sharing setup and permissions meet Apple's requirements. Test enforcement on a physical device. A Simulator or ineligible account may not complete child authorization.

Changing language

Open Parent controls > Settings > App language and choose Follow system, Simplified Chinese or English. App copy changes immediately and the choice is remembered. System authorization dialogs use the language managed by iOS.

Forgotten parent PIN

Reset it with the recovery code saved during setup or a later replacement. If you also lost that code, contact support for troubleshooting guidance. We cannot read your original PIN or recovery code or promise remote removal of restrictions. Do not send these credentials to support.

Remote management

The current app does not offer remote pairing, location tracking or combined usage totals across devices. Install, authorize and configure each device separately.

Reporting a problem

Email arxivchat@gmail.com with the device model, OS version, TimesUp version, steps and error message. Redact sensitive information from screenshots. Do not send children's names, parent PINs, recovery codes or Apple Account passwords. For a purchase issue, provide only the order information needed to help.
