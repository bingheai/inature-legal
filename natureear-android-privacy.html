NatureEar for Android — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

NatureEar is published by Nanjing Zhuoben Technology Co., Ltd (ZhuoBen Studio). This policy explains how the Android app handles information.

Offline sound recognition and local records

NatureEar uses microphone access to identify bird and wildlife sounds with an on-device model. Microphone audio is processed on your device for recognition and is not uploaded to us for identification. Discovery history, saved species, preferences and identification-credit balances are stored on your device. Recognition results and species details are not guaranteed to be accurate.

Usage analytics and diagnostics

The release app uses PostHog to understand app use and improve reliability. This may include an SDK-generated identifier, device and app information, language, screen names, app lifecycle and feature interactions, error information and purchase-related events. Connection information such as an IP address may be processed by service providers and may be used to derive an approximate location. We do not request precise location permission for sound recognition.

Session replay is enabled in the Android release configuration. It may record screen representations, interactions and diagnostic information. The SDK applies its default masking to sensitive inputs and images, but masking is not a guarantee that all sensitive information is excluded. Please avoid entering sensitive personal information into the app.

Purchases

Google Play Billing and RevenueCat provide optional one-time purchases, purchase restoration and entitlement management. They may process app user identifiers, device and app information, product identifiers, purchase history and transaction details. We do not receive full payment card details. Payment information is handled by the payment provider.

Optional Google Drive synchronization and sharing

If you enable Google Drive synchronization, Google authorization is used to access the app-specific storage area of your Drive. Saved discovery metadata is transferred to and from that area so that you can synchronize favorites. This feature is optional; local sound identification does not require an account. Google handles account authorization under its own policies. If you share a discovery, the information you choose is sent to the app or service you select. External species links open third-party websites whose policies apply.

Permissions and choices

Microphone permission enables sound recognition. Foreground-service and notification permissions support ongoing listening and its notification. You can stop listening or change permissions in Android settings. You can use the core local features without enabling Drive synchronization. The app does not use an advertising network to display third-party ads.

Retention, deletion and privacy requests

Local records remain until you delete them or remove app data, subject to Android backup settings. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete copies in your Google Drive or provider records. You can manage app data in your Google Drive account and revoke the app's access in Google Account settings. For deletion of analytics or purchase-related records held by us or our processors, contact arxivchat@gmail.com. Provide enough information for us to identify the relevant records; do not send passwords or full payment details. Some transaction records may be retained to meet legal, fraud-prevention or accounting obligations. Provider retention and deletion rules also apply.

Families and children

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use, device permissions, sharing and purchases. If you believe information relating to a child has been processed inappropriately, contact us so that we can investigate and address the request.

Security and transfers

Remote providers use encrypted network connections. Information may be processed outside your country according to the providers' locations and policies. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Contact and updates

Privacy contact: arxivchat@gmail.com. We may update this policy when features or data practices change. The date above identifies the current version.
