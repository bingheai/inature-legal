Delete your MrRock data

MrRock does not require an account. This page explains how to delete data stored by the app and how to request deletion of data processed by our service providers.

1. Delete data stored on your device

Open MrRock, go to Settings, and delete individual scan history items or use “Clear All History.” Saved collection items can be removed from the collection. Uninstalling MrRock removes remaining local app data, subject to your device backup settings.

2. Request deletion of analytics data

MrRock uses PostHog for product analytics. Email arxivchat@gmail.com with the subject “MrRock data deletion” and include the app user or device identifier shown in the app if available, or enough detail to identify the relevant analytics records. We will delete or anonymize the associated analytics data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

3. Purchase records

Purchases are processed by Google Play and RevenueCat. MrRock does not receive your full payment-card details. Google Play and RevenueCat retain transaction records under their own policies. Contact us if you need help identifying the relevant provider or exercising your rights.

Data that may be kept

We may retain limited records where required for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, or security purposes, and provider-held transaction records according to the provider’s policy. We do not sell personal information.

For privacy questions, see the MrRock Privacy Policy or email arxivchat@gmail.com.
