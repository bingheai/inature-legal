Contact

Email arxivchat@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, Simple TunerX version, and a description of the issue. Never send payment-card details or your Apple Account password.

Restore a Purchase

Confirm that the device uses the Apple Account that made the original purchase, then select Restore Purchase in Simple TunerX Settings. Apple handles refunds and billing questions.

Microphone Problems

Open iOS Settings — Privacy & Security — Microphone, confirm Simple TunerX has permission, and check whether another app is using the audio device.
