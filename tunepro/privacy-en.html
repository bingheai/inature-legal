1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how TuneProX (the “App”) handles information while providing instrument tuning, a metronome, chord references, custom tunings, and a lifetime in-app purchase.

2. Microphone and Audio

With your permission, the App reads live microphone audio to calculate pitch, stability, and tuning deviation on your device.

The App does not record or retain your performance audio and does not upload microphone audio to our servers. Audio analysis takes place on your device.

You can disable microphone access in iOS Settings. Tuning will then be unavailable, while the metronome, chord library, and settings remain usable.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following locally to provide its features:

  • Instrument, tuning, A4 reference, sensitivity, theme, sound, and haptic preferences;
  • Custom tunings you create;
  • Free-use state and selected metronome tempo and time signature;
  • A cached purchase-entitlement state.

Deleting the App generally removes this local data. Transaction records retained by Apple or a purchase service are governed by their own retention practices.

4. Purchases and Service Providers

The lifetime purchase is processed by the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your complete payment-card information.

The App uses RevenueCat to load products, validate transactions, and restore purchase access. For those purposes, RevenueCat may process device type, operating system, the last time the App was used, Apple transaction receipts, purchase history, and an anonymous app user identifier it generates. We do not provide RevenueCat with your name, email address, or advertising identifier through the App.

Please also review Apple's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

5. Use and Sharing

We process information only to provide tuning features, remember preferences, complete purchases, and restore access. We do not sell personal information, show advertising, or use microphone audio for advertising tracking.

We do not intentionally disclose information to other third parties except for Apple and RevenueCat as needed for purchases, or where required by law.

6. Retention and Your Choices

Local settings and custom tunings remain until you change them or delete the App. Apple and RevenueCat retain transaction information according to their policies. You can disable microphone, sound, or haptic features and can contact us with a privacy request.

7. Children

The App is not designed to collect personal information from children. Minors should use purchase features with a parent or guardian.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when features, providers, or legal requirements change. The revised version and date will be posted here.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact arxivchat@gmail.com.
