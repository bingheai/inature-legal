MrRock for Android — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Information processed on your device

MrRock uses photos that you capture or select to identify rocks and minerals. Recognition runs on your device. Scan history, saved discoveries, app settings, and related notes are stored locally on your device. You can delete records in the app or remove local data by uninstalling the app, subject to your device's backup settings.

Information sent to service providers

The app uses PostHog to measure app use and help improve the app. Depending on the feature and configuration, this can include app and device information, an SDK-generated identifier, app lifecycle and feature events, screen names, language, and purchase-related context. The Android configuration enables session replay, which may collect screen snapshots, touch interactions, and diagnostic logs. The SDK is configured to mask text inputs and images by default, although no masking system can guarantee that all sensitive information is excluded.

The app uses RevenueCat and Google Play Billing to display products, process and restore purchases, and manage entitlements. These providers may process app user identifiers, device and app information, and transaction and purchase information. MrRock does not receive your full payment card details. Google and RevenueCat process information under their own privacy policies.

Permissions and choices

Camera access is used to capture photos. Photo access is used when you select an image, and storage access may be used when you save or share a result. You may change these permissions in Android settings. If you use system share or backup features, the information you select is handled by the service you choose. MrRock does not require an account.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app, subject to system backup settings. Data processed by third-party services is retained according to their policies and the developer's configured retention settings. Contact us about privacy requests; we may need information sufficient to identify the relevant records.

Children

MrRock is not specifically directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information inappropriately, contact us.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, email arxivchat@gmail.com. We may update this policy as the app and its practices change; the latest published version will include its update date.
